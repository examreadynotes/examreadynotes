FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you buy — answered honestly.

What exactly do I get after purchasing? +
You get permanent access to the digital notes in your personal My Materials library. The notes are high-quality scans of handwritten pages — clear, readable, and ready to study from any device. No physical shipping involved.
How long does it take to get access after payment? +
Access is granted after payment verification, which typically happens within a few minutes. Once confirmed, your purchase appears instantly in your My Materials section — no manual steps needed on your end.
Do I need to create an account to buy? +
Yes — a free account is required so your purchases are saved securely and accessible in your personal library. Sign up takes under a minute and you only need an email address.
Will my access expire after some time? +
Never. Once you purchase a set of notes, you have lifetime access. You can return to your library a year later and everything will still be there — no subscriptions, no renewals.
Can I access the notes on my phone? +
Yes — the platform is fully responsive and works on mobile, tablet, and desktop. You can study from your phone wherever you are, including offline if you've saved pages for later (where supported).
Are the notes handwritten or typed? +
Every single note is genuinely handwritten — no AI-generated text, no typed and printed PDFs. They're written the way a dedicated student would prepare their own notes: concise, structured, and exam-ready.
What exams are the notes targeted at? +
Currently we cover JEE Mains, JEE Advanced, NEET, and Class 11–12 Board subjects (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology). We're constantly adding new subjects and exams — check the Free Resources section for a preview.
What if my payment went through but I didn't get access? +
This is rare but if it happens, email us at examreadynotes@gmail.com or use the Contact section on the main site with your payment screenshot. We'll manually verify and grant access within a few hours — guaranteed.
Is there a refund policy? +
Because these are digital products delivered instantly, we don't offer refunds as a general policy. However, if you received incorrect content or a technical issue prevented access, reach out and we'll make it right. We stand behind every product we sell.
Are there any free resources I can try first? +
Absolutely. We offer a growing collection of free notes, formula sheets, and quick-revision PDFs — no purchase required. Head to the Free Resources section on the main page to download them instantly.
Who is behind Exam Ready Notes? +
Exam Ready Notes was built and is run by a JEE Aspirant who personally creates all the content. There's no large team or corporation behind this — just a student who wanted better notes and decided to share them. That's why every product is made with real exam pressure in mind.
How do I contact support? +
You can reach us via the Contact form on the main site, or email directly at examreadynotes@gmail.com. We also respond to DMs on Instagram @examreadynotes_. Response time is typically within 24 hours.

Still have questions? We're happy to help — reach out any time and we'll get back to you within 24 hours.
